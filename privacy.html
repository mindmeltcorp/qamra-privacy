Qamra: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version. We never sell your data. Qamra has no ads and no advertising tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites. What you log in Qamra is saved in the app on your own device, and we cannot see it. If you choose to, you can share anonymous counts of how the app is used; this is off unless you turn it on, and never includes your health data.

Who we are

Qamra is made by the developer listed on its store page ("we"). You can reach us at mindmeltcorp@gmail.com.

What you put into Qamra

You may log period days, how you feel, notes, a pregnancy start date, missed and made-up fasts, dhikr counts, your first name and your settings. This is saved in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us, and we have no copy of it.

What leaves your device

  • Anonymous usage counts, only if you turn them on. During setup and in Settings > Privacy you can choose “Share anonymous usage”. It is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, the app sends short notes such as “opened the calendar”, “opened the plans screen”, “turned on the Friday reminder” or “the app was opened”, together with a random number made for this install, the app version, the phone model and system version, and the app language. It never sends your period days, cycle, pregnancy, moods, symptoms, notes, fasts, dua counts, dates you logged, your name or your contacts. There is no screen recording, and we do not keep your location. These counts go to PostHog, our analytics service, which stores them in the United States and processes them for us under its own terms. We use them only to see which parts of Qamra help and where people get stuck. You can turn this off at any time in Settings; the app then stops sending anything and forgets the random number.
  • Ramadan dates. The app downloads a small public file with announced Ramadan dates. Nothing about you is sent with that request. Like any download, the server that hosts the file (GitHub) can see your internet address.
  • Purchases. If you buy a plan, the payment is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We receive no card details. The app only learns whether a purchase is active.
  • Backups you make. If you save a backup file or a PDF summary, it goes only where you choose to save or send it.
  • Your device's own backup. If you have turned on your phone's backup (iCloud Backup, or Google backup on Android), the phone may include Qamra's data in that backup, under your own Apple or Google account. You control this in your phone's settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use advertising tools, and nothing in Qamra follows you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not share your health information with anyone.

Reminders, app lock and widgets

Reminders are created on your device and are not sent through our servers. App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint or face check; Qamra never sees your fingerprint or face data. Home-screen widgets show what you logged, on your own home screen.

Deleting your data

In Qamra, open More and choose "Erase everything", or delete the app. Both remove the data from your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Qamra is not directed to children under 13.

Not medical advice

Qamra is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and its estimates should not be used to prevent or plan a pregnancy.

Changes

If we change how Qamra handles data, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
